<objective>
Compose the messaging-spine: a NestJS DynamicModule that, when imported once by each service, registers every artifact built in waves 1-3 (entities, repositories, publisher, listener, decorator-host dependencies, dead-letter, CLS, topology) and asserts the RabbitMQ topology at application bootstrap. After this plan, a consuming service can do `MessagingSpineModule.forRootAsync({...})` in its AppModule and have the entire spine working.

Purpose: Without a single composition point, every service would have to wire 7+ providers manually and duplicate the topology-assertion logic. The module also enforces correct startup ordering — TopologyBootstrap and OutboxPublisher both implement OnApplicationBootstrap (not OnModuleInit) so they fire AFTER MikroORM is fully initialized (02-RESEARCH §Pitfall 5).

Output: TopologyBootstrap service + MessagingSpineModule + barrel update — service wiring (plan 02-07) consumes this.
</objective>
