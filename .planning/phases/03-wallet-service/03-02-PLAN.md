| T-03-04 | Tampering | Wallet balance invariant | mitigate | `Money.subtract` throws `NegativeMoneyError`; `Wallet.debit` catches and rethrows as `InsufficientFundsError` — fail-closed; secondary defence is the Postgres CHECK constraint in Plan 03-03 |

</threat_model>
