<objective>
Tighten the Kong route map so the wallets-service mutation surface is closed at the gateway. Today's `wallets-routes` block forwards everything under `/wallets/*` to the service; a curl to `POST /wallets/me/debit` (or any future mutation path) silently reaches the service and returns 404 there. After this plan, Kong itself returns 404 before the service is touched — a hard architectural signal that REQ-WALL-04 is enforced at the gateway layer.

Purpose: REQ-WALL-04 says debit/credit happen ONLY via RabbitMQ. The recruiter will look at Kong's route table during arguição. A permissive route map is a soft signal; a narrowed map is a deliberate one. The `kong.yml` change is declarative and verifiable in one `curl` per probe.
Output: Updated `kong.yml`, restart of Kong only (no other services), and a documented post-condition curl battery.
</objective>
