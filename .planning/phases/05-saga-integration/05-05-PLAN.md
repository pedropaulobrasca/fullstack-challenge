<objective>
Land the games-side AMQP consumers that close the saga loop. `WalletDebitedHandler` branches on `bet_saga_state.status` to either CONFIRM (transition Bet → ACTIVE) or COMPENSATE (emit `wallet.command.credit` to restore balance — research Pattern 3). `WalletDebitRejectedHandler` refunds the Bet. Both use `@IdempotentSubscribe` so inbox dedupe + txEm propagation come for free from the spine.

Purpose: REQ-SAGA-01 step 2 (the receiving end of the wallet round trip). The compensation branch is the trickiest correctness point in Phase 5 — it ensures that a slow `wallet.debited` arriving AFTER the sweeper refunded the bet still restores the player's balance via a downstream credit. Inbox dedupe in Wallet (Phase 2) prevents the original debit from being applied twice.

Output: Two handler classes, envelope types, module wiring, unit tests with stub repos covering all FSM branches.
</objective>
