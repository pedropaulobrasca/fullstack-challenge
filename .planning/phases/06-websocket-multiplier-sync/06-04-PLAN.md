Ship `MultiplierBroadcastService` — the 30 Hz recursive-setTimeout loop that calls `RoundLoopService.getMultiplierAt(now)` and broadcasts `round:tick` to the `lobby` room via `volatile.emit` (REQ-WS-06). Output: `MultiplierBroadcastService` with `start(roundId)` / `stop()` methods, registered in `GameCoreModule`. Unit test covers idempotent start/stop, tick payload shape, defensive handling of `getMultiplierAt` throwing mid-tick.
